# Batch 6 Report - Applied Labs 20-24

Date: 2026-08-22

## Outcome

Five original applied workflows are functional, local-first, documented, tested, and lazy-loaded from stable Applied Labs routes. Each project serves a named real-world user and produces a reusable decision, contract, drill, or evidence artifact rather than a generic calculator, visualizer, checklist, or quiz.

## Delivered labs

20. LLM Data Contract Firewall - converts field-level classification, purpose, and retention rules into fail-closed handling decisions, a least-data pipeline, leakage gates, JSONL schema, and dataset-card contract without ingesting records.
21. Shortage Response Trade-off Lab - compares candidate-price and purchase-cap allocation policies using revenue, served buyers, unmet demand, spend burden, access coverage, and segment-gap evidence, then emits a neutral owner memo with a local-law review gate.
22. LP Exit Rehearsal - models a synthetic full-range constant-product liquidity exit, hold benchmark, impermanent loss, fee break-even, curve price impact, fee-inclusive execution gap, stop triggers, and governance memo without a wallet or live data.
23. Account Recovery Drill Composer - detects correlated device, phone, email, notification, and shared-method recovery failures and generates a no-secret tabletop package, emergency cards, and owned next actions without contacting a provider.
24. Feature Misuse Contract - turns one startup customer promise and its trust boundaries into five to seven prioritized defensive misuse stories, observable negative acceptance tests, evidence owners, OWASP mappings, and a copyable pull-request contract.

## Limitations

- Field contracts and generated security tests require review by the organization that owns the real system.
- The shortage model uses simplified segment inputs and is neither pricing nor legal advice.
- The LP model is a simplified full-range x*y=k scenario and excludes live markets, concentrated liquidity, accumulated fees, gas, taxes, token behavior, and contract risk.
- The recovery drill does not validate actual provider recovery readiness.
- OWASP mappings organize a defensive review and do not prove vulnerability coverage.
- Final visual design remains deferred until all functional lab batches are complete.
